System Instructions & Help Guide

Comprehensive guide to navigating the Sunrise Dental Clinic Management System.

Receptionist & General Staff Operations

1. Registering an Appointment

Navigate to Register Appointment. The Appointment Number is auto-generated for accuracy. Select the relevant Dentist and Treatment from the dynamically updated lists. The system automatically prevents double-bookings for the same time and doctor.

2. View/Search Appointments

Use View/Search Details to see all scheduled, completed, and cancelled appointments in one place. You can also search for a specific patient's appointment using the exact ID (e.g., APT-2026-0001).

3. Clinic Info & Pricing

Check the Clinic Info & Pricing tab to quickly answer patient inquiries regarding available dentists, treatment descriptions, and accurate total estimated costs.

4. Calculating & Printing Bills

Enter the Appointment Number in the Calculate & Print Bill section. The system safely merges consultation fees with exact treatment costs to generate an invoice. Click Print Receipt to generate a clean, paper-ready bill for the patient.

Administrator Features (System Admins Only)

System Management (Dentists, Treatments, Staff)

Admins have exclusive access to manage clinic records from the sidebar. You can Add, Edit, or Delete Dentists, Treatments, and Staff members. Note: Deleting a dentist or treatment safely archives them (Soft Delete) to protect existing patient appointment records.

Reschedule or Cancel Appointments

In the View/Search Details section, Admins will see exclusive "Edit" and "Cancel" buttons next to all appointments to manage clinic schedules and handle patient cancellations directly.

Security Warning

Always click the Exit System button from the left menu before leaving the front desk to ensure unauthorized personnel cannot access patient records or administrative controls.
